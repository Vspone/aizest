Submagic is an AI video editor aimed squarely at short-form creators — the people publishing to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts on a weekly cadence. Rather than trying to be a full non-linear editor, it does one job well: take raw footage, caption it automatically, style it to look native to the platform, and export a vertical video in a few minutes.

## What it actually does

The core loop is upload → auto-caption → style → export. Submagic's automatic transcription generates word-level timing, then applies animated caption presets that highlight the current word as it is spoken. Presets are the part creators notice first, because that styling is what makes a clip feel native to Reels and Shorts rather than like a subtitled export.

On top of captions, the editor includes auto zooms, silence removal, bad-take removal, filler-word cleanup and audio cleanup. The Pro tier adds a Storyblocks b-roll and sound-effects library, so you can break up a talking-head clip without leaving the app.

## Magic Clips

Magic Clips is the feature that distinguishes Submagic from caption-only tools. You feed it a long recording and it returns several vertical clips with captions already applied, ranked by likely interest. It is a real time saver for podcasters and YouTubers who want shorts out of an existing episode, though the quality of the selection tracks the quality of the source — clean, single-speaker audio with clear topic changes produces noticeably better clips than noisy multi-person recordings.

Credits are the real constraint: Starter includes 45 credits per member (15 videos or 5 Magic Clips), Pro 120 credits (40 videos or 15 Magic Clips), Business 300 credits (100 videos or 37 Magic Clips). Yearly billing saves up to 41%.

The pricing page lists paid tiers only and advertises "no credit card required" to start, so budget for a paid seat if you publish consistently — this is not a permanent free tool.

## Who it fits

- **Solo short-form creators** publishing several clips a week: Starter is priced to work at that volume.
- **Podcasters and YouTubers** repurposing long episodes: Magic Clips on Pro is the sweet spot.
- **Small teams and agencies** running multiple client accounts: Business unlocks 4K, 60 FPS, custom templates and multi-account publishing.

It is a poor fit if you need a general-purpose editor with multi-track compositing, colour grading or motion graphics — that is not the problem Submagic solves.

## Bottom line

Submagic is a focused, fast caption-and-shorts tool with an above-average entry tier and a clear upgrade path. The main caveat is per-member pricing and the credit allowance, which together define how much video you can actually ship each month.
