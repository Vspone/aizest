Zapier is the reference implementation of no-code automation: a trigger in one app, an action in another, connected without writing code. The platform now supports 9,000+ integrations — the largest catalogue in the category — and bundles its own database (Tables), form builder (Forms) and an MCP layer that lets AI assistants take actions across connected apps.

The pricing model is the part that decides whether Zapier fits a small business. Every step in a Zap consumes a task, and AI steps, Code by Zapier and SDK calls all draw from the same pool rather than separate budgets. Free includes 100 tasks a month and two-step Zaps only; Professional starts at $19.99/month with multi-step Zaps, unlimited premium apps and a selectable task tier that scales up to 2M tasks; Team starts at $69/month for 25 users with shared Zaps and SAML SSO. Polling for new data, filters, formatters and paths do not count as tasks, and failed actions do not consume quota — details that matter when you are estimating monthly volume.

For freelancers and small teams the practical pattern is to prototype on the free tier, then price the paid tier by counting steps × runs per month rather than by the sticker price. A three-step Zap running 200 times a month already needs roughly 600 tasks, which is why the jump from 100 free tasks to a paid tier usually happens in the first week of real use.
